# PERL synthetic Progress Review contract

Contract: `perl-synthetic-progress-review/1.0`  
State schema: 25  
Event schema: `schemas/progress-review-event.schema.json`  
Current package export: `perl-synthetic-calibration-package/2.21`

## Why this exists

The e-QPASS materials say an assessment can be repeated at appropriate intervals to track findings over time and validate treatment plans. PERL meeting notes then name a multi-assessment comparison tool with AI-generated summaries, visual charts, refreshed recommendations, and affirming language as a desired expansion. Progress Review implements the narrow provider-facing foundation that the current evidence can support: transparent comparison of raw scored signals across two synthetic readings, a deterministic conversation brief, and an additional printable rehearsal page.

It does not implement treatment recommendations, make a progress judgment, or treat a score delta as a clinical outcome.

## Frozen rehearsal series

`FF-TEST-SERIES-01` is a deliberately constructed pair:

| Marker | Fixture | Display label |
|---|---|---|
| Point 01 | `FF-TEST-2388-B` | Earlier synthetic reading |
| Point 02 | `FF-TEST-2411-C` | Later synthetic reading |

These records are not asserted to belong to the same person, episode, provider, or treatment course. Their pairing exists only to rehearse the interface and evidence contract. No real dates are shown. The series fingerprint binds the point order, contract version, and both scored-source hashes.

## What the surface shows

- exact Point 01 and Point 02 raw scores for depression, anxiety, anger, and global distress;
- instrument maxima, deterministic level labels, signed raw deltas, and direction labels such as `raw score lower`;
- like-label subscales present at both points;
- deterministic critical-screen route for each point;
- a generated comparison summary that repeats the four exact raw movements and denies an outcome interpretation;
- an affirming opening prompt and four evidence-linked provider conversation priorities;
- a structured next-question form and immutable observation history;
- the source-series fingerprint and observation-chain head.

The initial frozen plate has core deltas of −39, −15, −12, and −73 respectively. Those numbers describe the fixtures. They do not mean “improved.”

## Generated Conversation Brief

`perl-synthetic-progress-conversation-brief/1.0` is generated by `deterministic-progress-baseline` version `progress-brief-0.1.0`. It uses rules only, transmits nothing to an external provider, and binds its summary, opening, four priorities, exact movement evidence, source routes, and denied claims in a stable fingerprint.

The brief starts with the person's account, then asks the provider to examine function, context, strengths, source comparability, and safety. These are conversation priorities—not refreshed treatment recommendations, a progress note, a diagnosis, or a care-plan instruction. The summary describes scores as higher or lower and explicitly says that the constructed pair is not evidence of improvement, deterioration, or treatment response.

## Printable progress addendum

`perl-synthetic-progress-addendum/1.0` renders the same bounded brief as a responsive, Letter-oriented HTML page. It includes the exact four movement rows, four conversation priorities, both safety routes, source and brief fingerprints, and a paired `can show / cannot conclude` boundary. Its visible footer states that it contains no PHI, is not a progress note, and is not approved for live clinical use. Printing the page does not create an approved clinical artifact.

## Structured observation only

The POST input accepts exactly four fields: `seriesId`, `focus`, `finding`, and `disposition`. All values come from fixed enums. There is no narrative field, patient note, raw response, Findings content, name, credential, or identity field. Each event pins the series fingerprint, both scored-source hashes, four core deltas, and both critical routes before entering a SHA-256-linked append-only history.

## Claim boundary

The status object and every event hard-code the following to `false`:

- authoritative subject linkage;
- patient or clinician identity resolution;
- clinical progress, improvement, or deterioration;
- treatment response, reliable change, or meaningful change;
- care-plan change or accepted clinical decision;
- clinical validation;
- pilot, production-release, or patient-use authorization.

The comparison does not diagnose, prescribe, establish causality, or replace direct review of the source evidence, safety screens, timeline, treatment exposure, functioning, medical context, or licensed clinical judgment.

## API

- `GET /api/progress` — live synthetic Progress Review status
- `POST /api/progress/observations` — record one enum-only rehearsal observation
- `GET /api/progress.json` — download the status, exact evidence, history, and integrity metadata
- `GET /api/progress/report.html` — open the read-only printable synthetic Conversation Brief

The full calibration package includes `progressReview`, `progressReviewEvents`, and `manifest.progressReviewEventChain`.

## Production replacement work

Before this workflow can compare real assessments, Focused Future and the e-QPASS owner must approve an authoritative longitudinal identity and episode contract; permissible dates and minimum-necessary fields; missingness and version compatibility rules; interval interpretation; safety escalation; reliable/meaningful-change methods; treatment-exposure and context capture; role-based access; PHI handling; retention; audit; human review; accessibility; and independent validation. A qualified clinical and statistical group must decide when raw changes are comparable and what, if anything, they support. The local synthetic series cannot supply those decisions.
