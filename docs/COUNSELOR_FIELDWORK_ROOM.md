# Counselor Fieldwork Room

Current package export: `perl-synthetic-calibration-package/2.36` (Room introduced in `2.31`)  
State schema: `sandbox-state/42`

## Why this surface exists

Dolores’s August 13, 2026 update makes counselor review and training a provider-side product requirement. The earlier January 12 product update asks for live reviewer accuracy feedback, a feedback loop, further testing, and independent review before stronger claims. The July 2026 proposal adds two to three guided sessions, report samples, counselor interpretation notes, and language and tone guidance. The Counselor Fieldwork Room gives that work a clear front door.

The room is a workflow projection over existing governed evidence. Package 2.33 added a bounded source-only Reference Drafting Room and its thirty-sixth integrity family. Package 2.34 added the actor-aware [Counselor Reference Adjudication Antechamber](./COUNSELOR_REFERENCE_ADJUDICATION.md) and thirty-seventh integrity family. Package 2.35 adds the [Counselor Reference Decision Docket](./COUNSELOR_REFERENCE_DECISION_DOCKET.md) and thirty-eighth integrity family: four ordered, separately keyed external decisions bound to the exact local dossier. It does not add an identity store, roster, scheduler, meeting record, transcript, credential record, training-completion control, local signing, key provisioning, clinical validation, trial execution, release, traffic, or patient-use control.

Package 2.36 preserves that provider-side boundary and connects only the exact verified reference freeze to the separate [Independent Review Admission Docket](./INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_ADMISSION_DOCKET.md). Seven further independently signed duties can make the exact review protocol execution-ready; they cannot record a result or establish performance.

## Experience model

The interface uses five visible moves:

1. **Review.** Open the priority synthetic summary and verify the generated language against scored evidence and deterministic safety routing.
2. **Draft from source.** Enter the [Reference Drafting Room](./COUNSELOR_REFERENCE_ROOM.md), where generated language and the frozen holdout are absent, and write an evidence-bound human reference candidate from scored development evidence only.
3. **Review disagreement.** Enter the Adjudication Antechamber after authoring. Compare anonymous sealed candidates, inspect structural overlap, and preserve dissent without voting or accepting a reference.
4. **Return external decisions.** Use the Reference Decision Docket to issue one exact challenge and verify independent authorship, language/safety acceptance, case decisions with dissent, and protocol freeze under four distinct outside keys.
5. **Compare model and reference.** Enter the blinded calibration workspace and judge evidence fidelity, clinical restraint, usefulness, correction burden, and workflow time without authorship cues.
6. **Record.** Use the enum-only Counselor Session Notebook to carry, revise, defer, or stop a fixed decision with an evidence source and optional synthetic case reference.

The guided runway mirrors the three-session Counselor Lab contract:

- Language + safety;
- Evidence + workflow;
- Freeze + handoff.

Every session card shows its local rehearsal state and notebook coverage. Each action moves to an existing controlled surface; no parallel or weaker write path is introduced.

## Evidence and readiness

The room reads current live values from the calibration metrics, denominator-first analysis, Counselor Lab, Counselor Session Notebook, safety incident control, Intended Use Charter, Language Review Office, and Provider Activation Workbook. It presents:

- frozen synthetic case count;
- synthetic summaries awaiting review;
- fixed notebook decisions observed;
- blind comparisons against the predeclared minimum;
- structured feedback entries;
- unresolved high-severity or critical stopping events;
- named counselor-panel, source-sample, intended-use, language, provider-rehearsal, and independent-evaluator readiness.

Local working evidence and outside authority are visually separated. A current local provider-rehearsal design is labeled local; roster, acceptances, source authority, and independent evaluation remain labeled outside.

## Minimum-necessary boundary

The room may show bounded ratings, correction categories, workflow friction, evidence pointers, a visibly synthetic case reference, and a fixed disposition. It never captures counselor names or credentials, roster, attendance, transcript, narrative session notes, patient content, raw responses, Findings content, PHI, training completion, clinical acceptance, protocol acceptance, independent-review decision, pilot authority, production authority, or patient-use permission.

An unresolved stopping event changes the visible safety state to stopped. The room cannot locally clear that event or override the existing safety control.

## Design and accessibility

The experience uses Focused Future’s existing warm paper, deep clinical teal, mint, rust, and restrained gold, with editorial typography and folio-like sequencing. It avoids generic AI gradients, chat patterns, decorative neural imagery, and dashboard clutter. The visual system reinforces the provider workflow: a dark briefing room, paper runway, source drafting desk, `A ≠ B / NO VOTE` adjudication casebook, outside-return rail, denominator ledger, and minimum-necessary boundary book.

All actions are native buttons with 44-pixel-or-larger targets and visible focus treatment. View changes move focus to the destination heading or first usable field. Live counts and safety changes are announced. The two-column evidence and boundary layouts collapse to one column at phone widths, the evidence pulse retains a readable two-by-two grid, and reduced-motion preferences disable animated scrolling within the surface.

## Still required before real counselor use

- named clinical lead and counselor roster, including qualifications, conflicts, and permitted-use attestations;
- approved de-identified authoritative Findings samples and attributable counselor references;
- accepted intended use, exact language, prohibited claims, disclaimer, and direct-review route;
- authenticated production identity and role mapping outside this sandbox;
- scheduled and facilitated training, attendance, accessibility accommodation, observation, and externally accepted completion evidence;
- independent evaluator, frozen protocol, denominator completion, accuracy and reliability review, and limitation register;
- privacy, security, legal, e-QPASS owner, clinical, site, release, traffic, and patient-use authority.

Nothing in package 2.35 claims that any of those external conditions is complete. A verified reference freeze establishes only the exact accepted counselor-reference set and independent-review handoff named in the four signed metadata returns.
