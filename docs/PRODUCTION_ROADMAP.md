# PERL production roadmap

This roadmap converts the working product slice into the staged build Dolores described. It is intentionally gated by evidence rather than calendar alone.

Execution ownership, working windows, security evidence, recovery requirements, and immediate decisions are maintained in [LAUNCH_READINESS_PLAN.md](./LAUNCH_READINESS_PLAN.md).

## Stage 1 — Product and clinical calibration

**Outcome:** counselors can review, edit, approve, and compare de-identified summaries.

- Use the [Intended Use Charter](./INTENDED_USE_CHARTER.md) to select one provider-first care-workflow context and align the clinician, care-coordination, payer/utilization, and operations formats before freezing legal language or choosing a pilot. Preserve all eight prohibited uses and obtain the five external acceptances; a local draft is not approval.
- Use the [Language Review Office](./LANGUAGE_REVIEW_OFFICE.md) to place the exact nine live clauses in front of the licensed clinical lead, counsel, privacy/security owner, executive/product sponsor, and e-QPASS owner. Resolve all six review questions against versioned copy; a local seal records the proof presented, not acceptance.
- Confirm the canonical structured Findings-report payload and score definitions.
- Use the aggregate-only [calibration intake map](./CALIBRATION_INTAKE_MAP.md) to reconcile the proposal’s source-reported assessment library before any record-level transfer: named authority, approved inventory, quarantine, minimum-necessary fields, quality/exclusion rules, counselor-reference provenance, strata, and governed holdout access.
- Run the source-backed [Counselor Lab](./COUNSELOR_LAB_PROTOCOL.md) as three separately gated sessions: language/safety, blinded usefulness/workflow, then reference/protocol freeze and independent handoff. Do not convert source-reported counselor availability or sandbox reviewer codes into a roster, attendance record, training claim, or acceptance.
- Use the [Counselor Session Notebook](./COUNSELOR_SESSION_NOTEBOOK.md) during local preparation to walk all fifteen fixed decision questions and bind enum-only observations to the evidence available at that moment. It is not attendance, a transcript, or a decision record; governed sessions still require authenticated participants, approved note policy, and signed outputs.
- Operate the [Counselor Reference Decision Docket](./COUNSELOR_REFERENCE_DECISION_DOCKET.md) only after every development case has a current sealed local comparison. Provision its four distinct purpose keys outside PERL, route the exact 24-hour challenge through an approved decision channel, and return independent-authorship, language/safety, case-decision/dissent, and freeze/handoff attestations in order. A verified freeze advances the exact reference set to independent review; it is not accuracy, reliability, clinical validity, or permission to run a trial.
- Resolve the GPI formula/threshold authority noted in earlier clinical-summary work.
- Convert counselor edits into a defined error taxonomy: factual mismatch, overreach, omission, tone, safety, usefulness.
- Run 2–3 recorded working sessions with Dolores and the beta panel.
- Create gold-standard notes for a stratified set covering low, mixed, elevated, and critical-screen profiles.
- Approve and freeze a machine-readable case manifest with eligibility, development/holdout partition, strata, source/reference versions, and governed holdout access.
- Decide what must remain deterministic: scoring, thresholds, critical-screen routing, identifiers, versioning, and export formatting.
- Use the completed metadata-only [Owner Return Desk](./EQPASS_OWNER_RETURN_DESK.md) to bring back the two exact Mike workbooks plus the scored-event, field-dictionary, version, lifecycle, attachment, and security artifacts without ingesting source content; then use the synthetic inbound-event, automatic review-routing, clinician-gated preparation, durable outbox, disabled connector, strict acknowledgement, bounded retry/dead-letter, and interrupted-attempt recovery rehearsal to resolve the authoritative e-QPASS contract, codebook, source-level semantics, Findings lifecycle, production worker/alert behavior, PDF/merge interface, attachment acknowledgement, and rescore/supersession rules.
- Use the [Model Trial Bench](./MODEL_TRIAL_BENCH.md) to name exactly three candidates against the same privacy, security, technical, clinical-evaluation, operational, and governance standard; then use the completed model-agnostic gateway and startup-controlled [HTTPS model transport bridge](./MODEL_TRANSPORT_BRIDGE.md) to compare them under the identical scored-input, structured-output, policy, snapshot, and regression contract. Provision one separately scoped policy/credential per candidate, keep activation synthetic-only, and select using accuracy, correction burden, safety, privacy terms, regional availability, observability, latency, cost, and signed ownership—not prose quality alone or local metadata completeness.
- Use the [Candidate Trial Foundry](./CANDIDATE_TRIAL_FOUNDRY.md) as the coordinator contract for that comparison: preserve the nine candidate runs, twelve balanced blind cells, six ordered measures, and seven permission gates. Use the [Candidate Return Desk](./CANDIDATE_RETURN_DESK.md) only to receive exact, current, structured synthetic outputs after the three-candidate metadata preflight; it supplies the immutable output ledger but not proof that a run occurred as declared. Replace manual external execution with three approved synthetic transports, then add a separately governed balanced blind-review intake for authenticated counselor ratings, corrections, dissent, and reviewer burden. A planning snapshot or structural receipt is not outcome evidence.
- Use the [Independent Review Dossier](./INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_DOSSIER.md) to hand a named conflict-disclosed evaluator the frozen six-domain question, controlled-input register, exact evidence denominators, runtime provenance, and ledger heads. A local dossier seal is not the outside decision.
- Use the [Independent Review Admission Docket](./INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_ADMISSION_DOCKET.md) to freeze the exact evaluator, case set, analysis plan, permissions, and protocol before results; then use the [Candidate Retest Independent Disposition Chamber](./CANDIDATE_RETEST_DISPOSITION_CHAMBER.md) only after one exact same-case X/Y cycle is complete. Provision seven admission keys and four result keys outside PERL under distinct hardware-backed custody. The four result duties may freeze exact-cycle accuracy, reliability, Clinical Standard satisfaction, and a recommendation, but cannot themselves close the cycle, select an engine, or authorize release.

**Exit evidence:** named counselor panel and clinical lead sign the Session 01 language/safety outputs, complete the governed evidence session, and sign the reference/protocol freeze and beta test plan for independent review.

## Stage 2 — Reliability study and pilot controls

**Outcome:** Focused Future has defensible evidence that PERL is useful and fails safely.

- Run blind human-versus-model comparisons in the product.
- Execute the admitted independent analysis and return all four ordered result signatures through the disposition chamber. Preserve the content-free exact-cycle scope and route any close/continue and provider/model/version action through separate authenticated decisions.
- Measure domain agreement, inference support, critical omission rate, unsafe-language rate, correction burden, and median review time.
- Freeze numerator and eligibility-denominator definitions for every safety outcome before clinical results are inspected; distinguish repeated event counts from unique affected cases.
- Replace the completed synthetic two-condition timing rehearsal with an independent-evaluator allocation plan, authoritative Findings interface, representative de-identified cases, and matched unaided-synthesis versus PERL-assisted-review observations before making any time-saving claim. See [TIMING_STUDY_DESIGN.md](./TIMING_STUDY_DESIGN.md).
- Establish inter-rater agreement and explicit release/failure thresholds before seeing results.
- Use the synthetic study package and [predeclared beta protocol](./CLINICAL_BETA_PROTOCOL.md) to rehearse analysis before approved de-identified cases are introduced.
- Keep a frozen holdout set for regression testing.
- Use the sandbox refinement brief and change register to rehearse feedback clustering, evidence-pinned loaded-version proposals, frozen replay, advancement, and rollback. Freeze the production recurrence and eligibility rules before results are inspected. Replace every local hash-linked ledger with authenticated immutable events, approval identity, trusted timestamps, signatures, and deployment controls.
- Complete legal review of intended use, disclaimer, retention, reviewer responsibility, and pilot consent.
- Validate the clinician, care-coordination, payer, and operations/admin formats with their actual users; freeze a minimum-necessary disclosure matrix before production role mapping.
- Prepare incident, escalation, and model-change procedures.
- Replace session-code incident resolution with authenticated clinical-lead restart authority, independent verification, immutable governed events, and notification/escalation integrations.

**Exit evidence:** independent clinical/reliability review approves a limited pilot.

## Stage 4 — Controlled pilot

**Outcome:** real-world utility and safety are demonstrated with bounded exposure.

- Start with the already-discussed partner clinic and university counseling setting only after agreements are signed.
- Require human review on every output.
- Monitor corrections, omissions, safety escalations, review time, user trust, and model drift weekly.
- Freeze changes during measurement windows and document every release.
- Conduct a stop/go review before expanding audience formats or automation.

**Exit evidence:** pilot objectives are met with no unresolved high-severity failure mode.

## Later — consumer PERL

The direct-to-consumer product remains a separate workstream. Reuse scoring, evidence, security, report infrastructure, and brand language where appropriate, but create a distinct intended-use statement, safety design, content-review process, and clinical/legal validation. Do not simply expose the clinician summary to consumers.
