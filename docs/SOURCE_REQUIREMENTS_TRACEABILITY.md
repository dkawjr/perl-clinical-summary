This is the build contract for PERL. It separates Dolores's actual product direction from proposal shorthand, earlier consumer concepts, and claims that still need evidence. ## Claims the product must not inherit blindly

PERL may move quickly. It may not collapse discovery, calibration, independent review, legal review, and production authorization into one green badge.
