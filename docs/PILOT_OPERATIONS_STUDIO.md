## Why this exists

The readiness ledger proves which local patterns are reproducible and which decisions remain external. The Decision Exchange gives each outside decision an exact request and metadata-return path. Neither surface previously answered a separate operational question: if a named provider site is eventually authorized, what exactly is the bounded pilot supposed to do?

The Pilot Operations Studio answers that question from Dolores’s January 12 and March 30, 2026 correspondence. It converts source-reported interest, timing, training, review, pricing, and provider-first direction into an inspectable working plan while refusing to turn an email statement into site authority.

## Source-reported pathways

The plan fixes two pathways and does not permit a locally invented third one.

1. **North Central University counseling-center candidate.** The source-reported working plan describes counseling-center use beginning in the academic year, an existing counselor caseload of approximately 50 on-campus students, an August training session, objectives fixed before use, an August–May ten-month term, and quarterly activity/feedback/data reviews. Group/dashboard access, a page customization or survey question, a working first-term price of `$750`, no setup/training fee, and a `$75/month` renewal reference remain provisional commercial assumptions. Dr. Brown and the Provost are preserved as a source-reported decision path, not authenticated authorities.
2. **Cooper University psychiatric-clinic QI candidate.** The source describes student quality-improvement interest and a diagnostic-agnostic baseline concept in which e-QPASS could be considered regardless of presenting pathology. No site, ethics, clinical, privacy, security, data, participant, or operating approval is inferred.

The first bounded product remains provider-side PERL beside the existing Findings report. Broad all-student access and consumer PERL stay deferred until provider-market evidence and a separately governed product decision exist.

## Operating spine

Four training modules are predeclared:

1. scope, intended use, role, prohibited uses, and unchanged Findings authority;
2. evidence-linked review, correction/return, and approved-artifact preservation;
3. critical-screen routing, stop conditions, incident escalation, downtime, and restart authority;
4. structured feedback, data quality, denominators, quarterly review, and change control.

Four decision moments prevent a pilot term from becoming an automatic rollout:

1. admission before any use;
2. first-quarter learning review;
3. midyear control review;
4. closeout and a new stop/revise/renew/consider-expansion decision.

## Measure before claim

Six measures are fixed before results:

- eligible workflow activity with an authoritative denominator;
- clinician review completion;
- correction burden;
- critical-screen routing adherence;
- review workflow time under a frozen comparison protocol;
- qualified-counselor usefulness ratings.

Each measure includes a guardrail. Generation cannot count as clinical review; low editing cannot establish accuracy; preference cannot establish reliability or patient benefit; and time savings require an accepted comparator and denominator. The Studio stores no pilot outcomes.

## Admission and closeout

The plan mirrors the seven external readiness decisions exactly: intended-use/language acceptance, authoritative e-QPASS contract, counselor calibration acceptance, independent reliability decision, Azure security/privacy acceptance, accessibility acceptance, and named-site pilot authorization.

The only closeout choices are stop, revise, renew, or consider a separately authorized expansion. Nothing renews, broadens, or becomes consumer-facing automatically.

## Persistence and API

`pilotOperationsEvents` is a separate SHA-256-linked evidence family. A snapshot pins the current plan fingerprint, source-backed counts, readiness state, Decision Exchange fingerprint, and six relevant chain heads. Startup fails closed if sequence, prior hash, plan fingerprint, counts, evidence context, denied claims, actor, time, note, or event hash is changed.

Endpoints:

- `GET /api/governance/pilot-operations`
- `GET /api/governance/pilot-operations.json`
- `GET /api/governance/pilot-operations.html`
- `POST /api/governance/pilot-operations/snapshot`

The HTML route renders an escaped three-page Letter brief: pathway and scope, operating spine and measures, then admission/closeout/commercial assumptions. The print artifact and main interface reflow for narrow screens and retain keyboard-visible focus.

## Claim boundary

A local seal proves which plan and evidence references were assembled. It cannot verify a site, caseload, counselor, licensure, authority, budget, agreement, training, e-QPASS access, data path, privacy/security acceptance, pilot participation, implementation, performance, outcome, renewal, expansion, production release, or patient use. It accepts no respondent records, Findings content, identities, files, credentials, or PHI. Every site-specific decision remains external.
